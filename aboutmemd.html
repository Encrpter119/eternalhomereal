The Journey

The Founding Vision

The story of Parah Traders and Eternal Home is rooted in a simple observation: the local market deserved access to international-standard architectural finishes without the friction of distant sourcing. Recognizing this gap, Mr. Shabir Parah established Parah Traders, aiming to supply premium building solutions directly to builders and homeowners in Kashmir.

Establishing the Showroom

As the enterprise expanded, the vision evolved into Eternal Home—a dedicated, boutique luxury showroom in Ganderbal. Today, the showroom stands as a landmark of design in Qadri Complex near Beehama, housing a premium collection of bathware, tiles, and fittings under one roof, guided by absolute transparency, trust, and premium service.
