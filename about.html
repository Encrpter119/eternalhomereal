Showroom Legacy

Setting International Standards

Founded with a vision to redefine modern architectural layouts in Ganderbal, Eternal Home has grown from a local supplier to Kashmir's leading showroom for luxury tiles and bathroom solutions. Located in the prominent Qadri Complex near Beehama PNB, we showcase a wide array of premium materials curated for sophisticated living.

We believe that a house becomes a home when every detail reflects the owner's personality. That's why we don't just sell products; we offer customized aesthetic consultations and layout planning, helping you find exactly the right match of designs, textures, and colors.

Whether you are building a custom hillside villa or renovating an urban kitchen, we offer the guidance, premium brands, and logistical support needed to make your construction journey seamless.
