FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide delivery throughout Ganderbal & Srinagar?

Yes, we offer fast and reliable delivery options across Ganderbal, Srinagar, and neighboring locations in Kashmir. Heavy loads of floor/wall tiles are shipped carefully to avoid transit breakage.

Which brands of tiles and bathware do you sell?

We are authorized showroom dealers for leading luxury brands, including Kajaria, Jaquar, Duragres, Somany, Cera, Johnson, Hindware, Parryware, and Orientbell.

Do you offer customization or design assistance?

Absolutely! We provide layout suggestions, size recommendations, color coordinating advice, and full architectural interior alignments to match your bathroom or living room layout specifications.

Can I place bulk orders for hotels or residences?

Yes, we cater to bulk supply inquiries for hotels, commercial towers, government complexes, and custom residential blocks. Contact our Ganderbal store manager at +91 7780877456 for corporate discount rates.
